The first version of emretufekci.com went up in March 2018, when I was a student in Cyprus. It had a background video of Istanbul, a few university club pages and some browser games. It's still in the repo's history (tag `v2018`) if you're curious.

A lot has happened since then. I graduated, spent six years building secure communication systems in the defense industry, did a master's on LLMs, started more side projects than I'd like to admit, switched jobs to work on voice AI, and got married.

This site is where I'll write about all of it:

- **RTC**: SIP, WebRTC, media, and voice agents. What I work on every day.
- **Engineering**: tools, automation, and things I build.
- **Startup**: honest notes from years as a technical co-founder.
- **Personal**: everything else.

The site is open source, a static site built with Eleventy. It deploys to my own server on every push. The [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/emretufekci/emretufekci.com), and you can subscribe via [RSS](/feed.xml).

Thanks for reading.
